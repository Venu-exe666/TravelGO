Prepare in four clear stages

The exact route depends on your nationality, residence, destination, and purpose of travel.

01

Select Destination

Confirm whether you need a visa and check the official rules for your nationality and country of residence.

02

Prepare Documents

Gather the application, passport, photographs, itinerary, finances, insurance, and any destination-specific evidence.

03

Review & Submit

TravelGO can help review completeness; submit through the official channel specified for your application location.

04

Decision & Travel

Wait for the authority’s decision, then verify the issued visa details and carry the required travel documents.

Important: Visa requirements can change and approval is never guaranteed. Confirm the latest rules with the embassy, consulate, or official immigration portal responsible for your application before paying or travelling.

Multi-Country Short stay

Schengen Tourist Visa

A Schengen short-stay visa can permit travel within the Schengen area, subject to the visa’s validity and permitted duration.

Typical preparation:
  • Passport valid at least 3 months after planned departure
  • Return Flight Ticket & Accommodation Voucher
  • Evidence of sufficient financial means
  • Travel Health Insurance (€30,000 minimum)

UAE 30/60 Days Tourist Visa

Travellers who are not eligible for visa-free entry or visa on arrival may need a tourist visa arranged through an eligible UAE channel.

Typical preparation:
  • Passport Color Scan (Clear Bio-Page)
  • Recent Passport Photograph (White Background)
  • Confirmed Return Airline Ticket
