01 Does the base package price include hotel accommodation?

No, unless the package explicitly says otherwise. Accommodation is normally selected as an optional add-on during booking, allowing you to choose the available hotel category that suits your plans.

02 What is included in a tour package?

Inclusions can differ between packages. Review the package’s destination, duration, price, flight details, and included-services information before reserving. Contact TravelGO when a listed inclusion needs clarification.

03 Can I browse destinations and packages without an account?

Yes. Destinations, active packages, general visa guidance, and contact information are publicly available. You may be asked to sign in or create an account when you continue into the booking workflow.

04 Does TravelGO guarantee a visa approval?

No. TravelGO can help organize and review application information, but requirements and final decisions belong to the responsible embassy, consulate, or immigration authority.

05 How do I start a package reservation?

Open Tour Packages, use the destination and category filters, then select “Reserve Package” on your preferred journey. The booking flow will guide you through the remaining details.

06 What happens after I send a contact inquiry?

Your message is added to the TravelGO staff inquiry inbox together with your name, email, subject, and travel requirements so the team can review and respond.
